Contributors
------------
``pyhctsa`` is an open, community-driven project. We are grateful to everyone who
has contributed code, documentation, bug reports, and feedback. A full list of
contributors is available on
`GitHub <https://github.com/DynamicsAndNeuralSystems/pyhctsa/graphs/contributors>`_.

Interested in contributing? See the :doc:`development guide <development/index>`
to get started.
